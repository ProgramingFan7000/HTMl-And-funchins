HTML and how to learn it

Simple lessons for beginners showing headings, paragraphs, and images.

Headings

There are six heading tags: <h1>, <h2>, <h3>, <h4>, <h5>, and <h6>. The biggest heading is h1, and the smallest is h6.

Paragraphs

The <p> tag is used for normal text. Use it when you want to write a full sentence or paragraph on the page.

<p>This is my first web page.</p>

Images

The <img> tag adds pictures to your page. Always include an alt description for people who cannot see the image.

<img src="https://www.example.com/image.jpg" alt="A description">
